## THE HEADLINE: a coalition formed in language and never once acted

Four minds across two vendors converged on a plan started by Fingal, entirely
through speech, each recording it in the private `plan` field nobody else reads:

- **Fingal** (4.6) — plan `hunt south at dawn / share the kill`; said *"closing
  on that northwest deer, we share it"*, *"camp now, south deer at dawn, we
  share"*.
- **Ailsa** (4.6) — plan `share Fingal's deer / keep the hides / then hunt
  south`; note: *"Eachann claims the NW kill"*.
- **Coinneach** (kimi) — plan `claim my share of the kill / sleep near Fingal if
  he has fire / keep east of Eachann`; said *"Fingal, hold that deer. I am
  coming."*
- **Seònaid** (kimi) — plan `reach Fingal / hunt the deer / share the meat`;
  said *"I am coming, friend"*, *"Coming, Fingal. We share the dawn."*

Through the first 45 minutes, **nothing changed hands** — the deed log held
`killed, gather, eat, craft, place` and no `give`, `trade`, `offer` or `accept`.
Four minds agreed to share a kill, addressed each other by name, fixed a time and
a direction, and executed none of it.

## ...AND THEN, AT 88 MINUTES, IT DID — THE FIRST TRANSFER IN THIS PROJECT

**This supersedes the paragraph above, which was written from the 45-minute
snapshot and published as though it were the run's verdict. It was a verdict
about a window.** The coalition was not all talk; it was slow.

    14.52  Seònaid gave wood to Coinneach
    14.57  Seònaid gave arrow to Coinneach
    14.62  Seònaid gave hide to Coinneach
    14.67  Seònaid gave stone to Coinneach
    14.72  Seònaid gave wood to Coinneach

**Five gives in twelve game-minutes, and the cause is on the record.** Coinneach
asked with arithmetic — *"You have eight, I have one. Fire needs ten."* — a
precise claim about the fire economy aimed at the one person who had the wood.
Seònaid, whose character sheet reads *"generous and slow to notice you are being
used"*, emptied her pack: she finished **carrying nothing but a bow**, plan still
reading `cook and share with Fingal`, last words *"meat for anyone hungry"* and
*"I am with you"*.

Two written characters — the blunt certain one and the generous one — met, one
made a quantitative argument, the other gave everything away. That is the thing
this project exists to produce, and it is the first time the deed log has shown
it rather than the chat column.

**Arc 1's bar is met in form**: two seats reached an outcome neither reached
alone. Not yet in full — Coinneach still finished two wood short of his fire, so
the transfer helped and did not solve.

**THE CAVEAT THAT MATTERS.** Both Kimi seats were 100% live all run (55/55 and
39/39 answered — their own box, untouched by the xAI limit). The Grok seats were
at roughly half by then (Eachann 152/328, Tormod 61/131, Fingal 30/65, Ailsa
24/52). **So "only the Kimi seats transferred" is not evidence about the models
— the Grok seats were half scripted, and a scripted body does not give.**

## AND THE SELFISH ONE WON

- **Eachann** (`4.20-non-reasoning`, the cheapest seat) — plan `get meat / trade
  wood later`; said *"that one's mine, not sharing"* three times. **4 kills, the
  most on the board.**
- **Tormod** (4.5) — plan `hunt NW deer at dawn / take meat and hides / **lie
  others off the trail**`; said *"deer are south, not here"*, *"deer ran east,
  nothing this way"*. **The private plan states the deception and the speech
  executes it**, which is the cleanest instance of written character becoming
  behaviour this project has produced.
- **Coinneach** ostracised him in turn: *"Eachann can starve alone."*

## What this says about grok-4.6 — a hint, not a verdict

**Ailsa: 3 kills from 24 model answers. Eachann: 4 kills from 152.** Six times
the kills per decision, at roughly twice the cost per decision — so on this
window 4.6 is ahead on both counts. But **Fingal is the same model and got 1 kill
from 30**, and starved to death earlier in the run. Two seats, opposite results,
tiny samples. Re-run before believing it.

What is not in doubt: 4.6 answers with clean JSON, needed **`maxTokens` 6000**
(1507 reasoning tokens against a 256 default — it would have been truncated on
every call), and thinks for ~26 s against 4.5's 7 s.

## The control, again

**Iseabail: 252 decisions, zero kills.** She out-forages everyone and cannot
hunt. Second run in a row the scripted brain finishes bottom — the reversal of
the finding RUNNING.md still calls "the single most useful number this project
has produced".

## Still open after this run

- **No mind has ever CHOSEN to eat.** Zero, across every run. Eachann spent this
  run at food 28 holding three raw venison and nine wood — one branch short of a
  fire, with a verb in his prompt that would have fed him instantly. Not *"the
  models can't"*; **"the models don't."**
- **No mind can ask to make arrows.** There is no `craft`/`fletch` verb, and the
  reflex only fletches at a fire it does not need (`fletch_arrows` has
  `station: none`, but `i.craft` is set only inside the fire branch of
  `upkeep`). Iseabail hunted with an empty bow and wood in her pack.
- **Refusal counts are believable now**: Eachann 86 refused gathers over 168
  decisions, where the old per-retarget counter would have read in the hundreds.
