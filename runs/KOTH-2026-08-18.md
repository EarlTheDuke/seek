# King of the hill — the first night, four matches, 2026-08-18

The mode was designed (PLAN-KOTH.md), built, and played the same evening, with
Jack on blue for every match. Each match found the next bug live, the fix
landed between whistles, and by the fourth the thing was a game: seven minds
across three vendors and a human, contesting one ring, with red calling focus
fire on the human by name. Raw logs local per the gitignore rule; the write-up
is the record.

## The four matches, and what each one taught

**Match 1 — 0–0, a draw nobody attended.** The clock started at SERVER BOOT
and burned its thirty minutes through a roster restart. Five properly-teamed
minds joined a match their briefs correctly said was over, and went hunting.
Fix: the state machine — 'waiting' until the first playing join; a watcher
does not trip the whistle. Also from this match: the roster allow-list was
silently dropping `team` (the INTENT_KEYS failure in roster form), and every
body spawned 359 m from the ring — Jack's whole report was "did not see bad
guys". Fixes: `loadRoster` carries `team`; joiners stand at their muster.

**Match 2 — BLUE won 14–0 at the cap.** The first completed match in the
project's history. Also shorter than designed: the cap formula divided by 60
twice, so "30 minutes" was 33 real seconds — caught in match 3 by the new HUD
compass rendering "RED won 2-0" seconds after the whistle. A thirty-minute
cap is 27.7 game hours, and matchcheck now asserts the arithmetic.

**Match 3 — blue 66, red 0 when it was put down.** The first match with real
scoring. Jack: "Followed Seònaid but she just wandered. Why?" — and the data
answered: her first decision was *"make for the hill — blue must score"*, and
`goTo` sent her ROAMING. The hill's name is a position phrase, not a
gazetteer entry: **the one goal the whole mode turns on had no reachable
noun.** Fix: "the hill"/"the ring" resolve to the ring while a match runs,
and the brief says so. (Her other lapse was one kimi timeout → scripted brain
→ 100 s of wander at her cadence — the known tinybox shape.)

**Match 4 — red 69, blue 19, CONTESTED when we called it a night.** The fix
worked instantly: twenty seconds after the whistle, Eachann was making for
the hill and Fingal rallied his side by name — *"Ailsa, Seonaid, on me to the
hill."* By the end red was coordinating against the human — *"Tormod: go for
Jack — clear blue off hill"*, *"Jack's mine — drive him"*, *"eyes on Jack
still — loose now"* — which is target-calling, and it emerged from two
characters and a score. Jack, mid-match: shots landed on the summit and were
refused downhill — the hill search only guarantees the CENTER clears the pvp
strangeness gate, and the noise field dips in pockets. Fix (landed for the
NEXT match): a war zone 3.5 radii around a running match's hill where shots
always resolve; the geography still rules the rest of the map.

## What the night proved

- **The mode works.** Teams, whistle, muster spawns, scoring, contested
  stalemates, the cap, respawn — all exercised live, matchcheck 20/20.
- **Minds play it.** Given a reachable noun and a fed start, both sides raced
  the ring, held it, rotated, and called targets. The scripted control played
  survival amid a war, exactly as a control should.
- **Team framing unlocks the social verbs.** Match 1's post-whistle economy
  produced the first `give` AND `accept` ever used by real models — after two
  purpose-built trade experiments produced zero. The verbs were never broken;
  the world had never given two minds a reason to be on the same side.
- **The conversation build holds at scale.** 394 said / 60 gagged in match 1's
  session; 162 said / 6 gagged deep in match 4. A season ago a whole run
  produced one sentence.
- **The human loop closes.** Jack asked "what direction do i walk" once; by
  match 4 the HUD compass answered it continuously, and that same compass
  caught the cap bug the checks had missed.

## Open items, in order

1. The war-zone fix has not yet been PLAYED (landed after match 4 started) —
   first thing to watch next session.
2. Kimi seats fail soft into wandering — a timeout at a 100 s cadence is two
   minutes of drift. Consider a match-mode fallback goal of "make for the
   hill" instead of "walk the country".
3. Iseabail ignores the match by design; decide whether the control should
   stay in match rosters or sit matches out.
4. Team colours on avatars — still the plan's deliberate v1 cut, still worth
   doing; "which one is red" cost Jack seconds in a fight.
5. `offer` and `guard` remain the only verbs never used by a model.
