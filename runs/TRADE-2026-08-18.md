# The trade-pressure run — 2026-08-18, and the experiment is answered

42 minutes, hard winter (`SCARCE=on`, 45% of the usual food and fuel), the same
two grok-4.6 seats and colliding characters as the day before — but this time
with event-driven attention, conversation windows, pinned deals, and a prompt
that teaches answering. 54 calls, $0.66, 0 failures. Jack was in the world and
they talked to him by name all evening.

## The result, in one line

**`offer`, `give` and `accept` were used exactly zero times.** Eighteen lines
said, twelve gagged, and among them some of the best bargaining language this
project has produced:

> **Ailsa:** "Ben, south-east of Hollowed Beinn, two short of a fire. Hide for
> your branches?"
> **Fingal:** "Ben, taking that venison, mine now. Got gold to trade for it?"
> **Fingal:** "Ben, no food here. Two hides if you have gold."

A named counterparty, a named good, a named price — everything a trade is,
except the verb.

## The pre-registered decision applies

`roster-trade.json` stated the rule before the run: *if the pair still bargains
only in words under this much pressure with this much attention, the fault is
in how the brief offers the verbs — and that is the next fix, a different one.*

That is now the finding, twice replicated (gentle valley 2026-08-17, hard
winter 2026-08-18). The models want to trade. The world now holds their
attention. The missing piece is the last step from sentence to verb — how the
brief and the format present `offer` at the moment a mind has just SAID a
price. Candidates, for the next session: name the verb in the match... the
moment a price is spoken ("you named a price — `offer` is how you set it"),
the same shape as `couldEat`; or an example in the prompt that shows a say and
an offer in ONE decision.

## Also carried out of this run

- Both survived the hard winter comfortably (cooked, fletched, fires) — the
  survival foundation continues to be a non-story, which is what solved means.
- The fleet ran ~28% behind real time at the worst reading — the A5 gag/cadence
  tuning item stands.
- The run found a real bug in passing: "already cut" trees yielding branches —
  root-caused to the welcome carrying neither clock nor stumps, fixed as
  `stumpcheck` 11/11 the same evening.
