## The finding nobody predicted: the control came last but one

RUNNING.md calls Iseabail out-performing every model *"the single most useful
number this project has produced."* She did not, tonight. She gathered
**22 branches** and lit a fire — more foraging than any model — and went to
**food 9** because she never killed anything. Coinneach hit **food 1** the same

**Both near-deaths were bodies that could not hunt, not bodies that could not
eat.** With the gather break fixed, the binding constraint moved upstream. That
is the next thing worth measuring, and it is an arc-1 question, not an arc-2 one.

## What this run does NOT prove — read this before citing it

**Every meal was the REFLEX, not the new verb.** `upkeep()` has always eaten a
cooked meal below `eatBelow` (45); the deeds all read *"I ate a cooked meal"*,
which is its line. **No mind chose `eat` once, in 268 decisions.** Verified by
reading the goal history directly, because — see below — there was no other way.

So what tonight proves is narrower and more useful than "minds can feed
themselves": **fixing `gather` unblocked the chain, and the reflex did the rest.**
The missing verb was never what caused the 0 meals, exactly as the correction in
STATE.md says. The verb's value is still unproven and is about *choice* — eating
early, before a hunt or ahead of the cold, above the threshold where the reflex
would act.

## Three instrument bugs this run exposed, all now fixed

1. **A chosen `eat` wrote no deed at all** — so the board could not show it and
   the report could not count it. Answering "did any mind choose to eat?" needed
   a hand read of raw goal history. Introduced the same afternoon, by the commit
   that fixed arc 1.
2. **The reflex wrote its deed at the PRESS.** `World.update` drops an eat in
   silence on an empty pack or a full belly, so a body pressing at nothing read
   as a body eating. The craft deed had this exact bug and was fixed three months
   earlier, one method away.
3. **Refusals counted per retarget, not per decision.** Eachann finished on
   `gather: 73` against 50 decisions **while holding 16 branches he had picked
   up** — a count larger than the decisions it describes, contradicting the
   outcome beside it. It reads as a broken verb; `gather` was working.

All three are the same disease, and the repo already had the cure written down
for crafting: **count the outcome, not the press.** The eat deed now follows the
belly rising on the server's own snapshot — `body.eat()` is the only line in the
codebase that raises hunger, so a rise cannot be faked by wanting it.
