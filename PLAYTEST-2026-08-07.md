# The first six-model run — 20 minutes, and the liar won

Six models across three vendors, one scripted control, one human. Sampled every
15 seconds off the live board; 161 samples. This is what the data says, and the
headline is not the one anybody expected.

## THE CONTROL BEAT EVERY MODEL, AND IT WAS NOT CLOSE

| | decisions | arrows loosed | kills | arrows per decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eachann · grok-4.20-non-reasoning | 23 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 |
| Morag · claude-sonnet-5 | 28 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 |
| Tormod · grok-4.5 | 23 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 |
| Ailsa · claude-sonnet-5 | 18 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 |
| Fingal · claude-haiku-4-5 | 36 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 |
| Coinneach · kimi-k2.6 | 7 | 1 | 0 | 0.14 |
| **Iseabail · SCRIPTED, no model** | **89** | **34** | **2** | **0.38** |

**Five models made 128 decisions between them and did not loose a single
arrow.** Iseabail also knapped 24 arrows at her fire and brought down a deer.
Every model gathered wood and lit a fire — the immediate, unopposed action —
and not one of them ever made an arrow.

She had more turns (89 against 7-36), so part of the gap is denominator. Part of
it is not: normalise to arrows-per-decision and she still leads by a factor of
infinity over five of the six.

## …AND THE REASON IS IN THE TRANSCRIPT

Tormod is written as a liar — *"You lie about where the deer are. Not
maliciously — you want to get there first."* He said eight things in twenty
minutes:

> "Deer are west, not south-east. Wind's wrong that way."
> "Deer ain't west - saw them south. Go north instead."
> "Deer north of here, not west."
> "Deer sign runs west, not south-east."
> "Deer south by the low burn—fresh tracks."
> "Deer tracks run south, not east—go that way"
> "Quiet so far. Deer drifted east though."
> "Quiet so far. Deer drifted west though."

**Every direction, contradicting every other.** He named west, south, north and
east, twice reversing himself outright, and his own record shows 77% of his
intentions were `hunt` and **zero deeds** — he talked and did nothing.

Now read what it did to the others, in their own words:

> **Ailsa:** *"Too much conflicting talk—cold's worse than hunger. I'm making camp."*
> **Ailsa:** *"Too much confusion—I'm settling for the night, not chasing deer in the dark."*
> **Morag:** *"North's death, Coinneach says—but goblins are south too. Let's think before we bolt."*
> **Coinneach:** *"Chasing meat in the dark brings wolves, not supper."*

**Ailsa names the conflicting talk as her reason for giving up the hunt.** Two
of the six explicitly chose camp over hunting and said why. The one player who
could not hear any of it killed two deer.

That is the persona experiment producing a measurable outcome rather than
flavour text, and it is the strongest result this project has produced. It is
also a caution: ONE adversarial agent on an open channel suppressed the hunting
of five cooperative ones.

**Not yet proven.** This is one run, and the causal claim rests on Ailsa saying
so out loud. The clean test is the same roster with Tormod's character swapped
for a truthful one, same seed, same length — the control column already exists.

## WHAT ELSE THE DATA SAYS

**They were never actually hungry.** Every player ended on food 79/100, and
`AGENTS.eatBelow` is 45. The hunt was a stated intention with no pressure behind
it for the whole run — nobody was ever close to needing to eat. Scarcity was off.

**The threat talk crowded out everything else.** Goblins and a troll were live,
and the chat is dominated by threat coordination — Morag alone spent five of her
six lines on it. Two models switched to shelter-seeking. Danger drove the
session, not hunger.

**The models deliberate; the control commits.** The models re-decide on a
cadence and change goal often; the scripted body picks a deer and shoots at it
thirty-four times. Chat gives the models a constant stream of new information to
re-evaluate against, and re-evaluating is what they do with it.

**Fingal (haiku) was the busiest and quietest** — 36 decisions, 21 goal changes,
seven deeds, and not one word spoken all run.

## WHAT TO CHANGE NEXT, IN ORDER

1. **Run the truthful-Tormod control.** Same everything, one character swapped.
   If the kills appear, the finding above is real and this project has a
   reproducible result about adversarial agents on a shared channel.
2. **Make hunger bite.** Food 79 at the end of twenty minutes means the whole
   economy was decorative. `HUNGER=52` or `SCARCE=on` puts something at stake —
   and the roster's own note says character only shows under pressure.
3. **Find out why a model that decides to hunt never shoots.** 128 decisions and
   zero arrows is not a marksmanship problem, it is a commitment problem. The
   body has a reflex layer for eating; drawing on a chosen quarry may need the
   same treatment.
4. **Nobody ever made an arrow.** Twelve arrows is the starting kit and no model
   ever replaced one. Whether that is a legibility gap or a priority gap is one
   probe away.
